# Tools of the Trade

> **Quern**  
> The **Quern** grinds grain into flour, oilseeds into oil and limestone into lime. It stays in the crafting grid and wears out slowly.

**Recipe (crafting table, shaped):** Stick + 2 × any stone crafting materials → Quern

> **Kitchen Knife**  
> The **Kitchen Knife** cuts dough into pasta, potatoes into fries and flax into fibre. Make it from flint or iron.

**Recipe (crafting table, shaped):** Flint + Stick → Kitchen Knife

> **Surveyor's Chain**  
> The **Surveyor's Chain** stakes out field plots of any shape and measures them in acres. See Surveying Plots.

**Recipe (crafting table):** 2 × Chain + 2 × Stick + Paper → Surveyor's Chain
