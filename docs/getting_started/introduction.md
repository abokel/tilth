# What Tilth Changes

Farming in Tilth follows real agronomy. Every block of ground has a **soil texture** (sand, silt, clay and their mixtures) that sets how much water it holds, how fast it drains and how well it keeps nutrients.

Crops grow on **thermal time**: warmth, not ticks. A cold spring slows them, a frost can kill them, and each crop has its own season.

Tilled soil remembers its **nutrients**, **pH**, **organic matter**, moisture and compaction, and every crop draws on them. Rain fills hollows into **ponds**, and water can optionally be made finite in the config.

Your own body needs a varied **diet**, and well-grown food is more nourishing.

Nothing here is meant to be a grind: every rate is in the config, and the commands under **/tilth** let you inspect everything. If something surprises you, look at it with the Soil Probe or **/tilth crop**: the reason is always shown.
