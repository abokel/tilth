# Selling at Market

> **Market Stall**  
> **Market Stalls** stand in many villages, or build your own. Right-click one with an empty hand for today's prices and news; right-click with goods to sell the stack for coins (sneak to sell one).

Money comes in steps of nine: nine copper **pennies** make a silver **shilling**, nine shillings a gold **crown**, and nine crowns a gold **sovereign**. Craft nine coins into the next one up, or one into nine of the coin below. The market pays in the fewest coins it can.

Prices move with the seasons: grain is cheap after harvest and dear before the next, hay dearest at the end of winter, milk cheap in the spring flush. **Events** push them further for a few days: a drought, a bumper harvest, a cloth fair, murrain among the herds, a hard winter.

Better produce pays more, from 60% for Poor up to 160% for Premium. Damp grain sells at a discount.

Now and then a **Travelling Farm Merchant** in a straw hat comes by. He buys produce at the day's prices and sells seed of named cultivars, veterinary draughts, compost and tack, then moves on after two days.
