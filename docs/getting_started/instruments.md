# Field Instruments

> **Thermometer**  
> Hold the **Thermometer** to read the air temperature: coldest at dawn, warmest in mid-afternoon. Use it for today's low and high, a frost warning and tomorrow's forecast.

**Recipe (crafting table, shaped):** 2 × Glass Pane + Redstone → Thermometer

> **Soil Moisture Meter**  
> Push the **Soil Moisture Meter** into soil, farmland or a crop's roots: water content, how much plants can still draw, whether it is waterlogged, and how many mm of water would refill the root zone.

**Recipe (crafting table, shaped):** Copper Ingot + 2 × Iron Ingot → Soil Moisture Meter
