# Oats

> **Oats**  
> **Oats** (_Avena sativa_), grass family.

- **Cool-season:** sow as soon as the ground thaws. Sprouts after 100 °C·d above 0 °C.
- Ripens after **1350 °C·d**. Best near 20 °C; stops above 33 °C.
- **Frost** kills it below -7 °C (-2 °C in flower).
- **Soil pH** 5.5-7 (tolerates 4.8-8). Roots 1.2 m. Some tolerance of wet soil.
- **Feeds on** N 85, P 15, K 90 kg/ha per crop.
- **Yield** when well grown: about 84 bu/ac (3 t/ha).
- **Harvest:** Oats and Oat Seeds.
