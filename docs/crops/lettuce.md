# Lettuce

> **Lettuce**  
> **Lettuce** (_Lactuca sativa_), daisy family.

- **Cool-season:** sow as soon as the ground thaws. Sprouts after 50 °C·d above 4 °C.
- Ripens after **650 °C·d**. Best near 18 °C; stops above 28 °C.
- **Frost** kills it below -3 °C (-2 °C in flower).
- **Soil pH** 6-7 (tolerates 5.5-7.8). Roots 0.4 m. Some tolerance of wet soil.
- **Feeds on** N 90, P 15, K 120 kg/ha per crop.
- **Yield** when well grown: about 312 cwt/ac (35 t/ha).
- **Harvest:** Lettuce and Lettuce Seeds.
