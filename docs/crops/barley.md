# Barley

> **Barley**  
> **Barley** (_Hordeum vulgare_), grass family.

- **Cool-season:** sow as soon as the ground thaws. Sprouts after 100 °C·d above 0 °C.
- Ripens after **1250 °C·d**. Best near 20 °C; stops above 34 °C.
- **Frost** kills it below -10 °C (-2 °C in flower).
- **Soil pH** 6.2-8 (tolerates 5.5-8.8). Roots 1.3 m. Dislikes waterlogging.
- **Feeds on** N 95, P 17, K 85 kg/ha per crop.
- **Yield** when well grown: about 74 bu/ac (4 t/ha).
- **Harvest:** Barley and Barley Seeds.
