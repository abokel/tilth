# Maize

> **Maize**  
> **Maize** (_Zea mays_), grass family.

- **Warm-season:** sow after the last frost, into warm soil. Sprouts after 60 °C·d above 10 °C.
- Ripens after **1350 °C·d**. Best near 30 °C; stops above 42 °C.
- **Frost** kills it below -1.5 °C (-1 °C in flower).
- **Soil pH** 5.8-7 (tolerates 5-8). Roots 1.5 m. Some tolerance of wet soil.
- **Feeds on** N 190, P 35, K 170 kg/ha per crop.
- **Yield** when well grown: about 143 bu/ac (9 t/ha).
- **Harvest:** Ear of Maize and Seed Corn. Grows two blocks tall.
