# Field Pea

> **Field Pea**  
> **Field Pea** (_Pisum sativum_), legume family.

- **Cool-season:** sow as soon as the ground thaws. Sprouts after 80 °C·d above 4 °C.
- Ripens after **1000 °C·d**. Best near 20 °C; stops above 32 °C.
- **Frost** kills it below -5 °C (-2 °C in flower).
- **Soil pH** 6-7.5 (tolerates 5.5-8.2). Roots 0.8 m. Dislikes waterlogging.
- **Feeds on** N 150, P 15, K 70 kg/ha per crop; fixes about 70% of its own nitrogen.
- **Yield** when well grown: about 45 bu/ac (3 t/ha).
- **Harvest:** Field Peas.
