# Potato

> **Potato**  
> **Potato** (_Solanum tuberosum_), nightshade family.

- **Warm-season:** sow after the last frost, into warm soil. Sprouts after 150 °C·d above 7 °C.
- Ripens after **1100 °C·d**. Best near 18 °C; stops above 30 °C.
- **Frost** kills it below -1.5 °C (-1.5 °C in flower).
- **Soil pH** 5-6.5 (tolerates 4.5-7.5). Roots 0.5 m. Dislikes waterlogging.
- **Feeds on** N 150, P 25, K 220 kg/ha per crop.
- **Yield** when well grown: about 312 cwt/ac (35 t/ha).
- **Harvest:** Potato.
