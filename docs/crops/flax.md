# Flax

> **Flax**  
> **Flax** (_Linum usitatissimum_), flax family.

- **Cool-season:** sow as soon as the ground thaws. Sprouts after 90 °C·d above 3 °C.
- Ripens after **1150 °C·d**. Best near 20 °C; stops above 32 °C.
- **Frost** kills it below -4 °C (-2 °C in flower).
- **Soil pH** 6-7 (tolerates 5-8). Roots 0.8 m. Some tolerance of wet soil.
- **Feeds on** N 70, P 12, K 60 kg/ha per crop.
- **Yield** when well grown: about 24 bu/ac (1.5 t/ha).
- **Harvest:** Flax and Flax Seeds.
