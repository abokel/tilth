# Onion

> **Onion Seeds**  
> **Onion** (_Allium cepa_), onion family.

- **Warm-season:** sow after the last frost, into warm soil. Sprouts after 110 °C·d above 6 °C.
- Ripens after **1500 °C·d**. Best near 20 °C; stops above 32 °C.
- **Frost** kills it below -6 °C (-3 °C in flower).
- **Soil pH** 6-7 (tolerates 5.5-7.8). Roots 0.4 m. Dislikes waterlogging.
- **Feeds on** N 120, P 22, K 120 kg/ha per crop.
- **Yield** when well grown: about 357 cwt/ac (40 t/ha).
- **Harvest:** Onion and Onion Seeds.
