# Common Bean

> **Dry Beans**  
> **Common Bean** (_Phaseolus vulgaris_), legume family.

- **Warm-season:** sow after the last frost, into warm soil. Sprouts after 50 °C·d above 10 °C.
- Ripens after **950 °C·d**. Best near 25 °C; stops above 35 °C.
- **Frost** kills it below -0.5 °C (0 °C in flower).
- **Soil pH** 6-7 (tolerates 5.3-7.8). Roots 0.9 m. Dislikes waterlogging.
- **Feeds on** N 120, P 15, K 80 kg/ha per crop; fixes about 40% of its own nitrogen.
- **Yield** when well grown: about 18 cwt/ac (2 t/ha).
- **Harvest:** Dry Beans.
