# Harvest & Quality

Harvest when the crop reaches its final stage: right-click it, or break it. Alfalfa and clover are cut at early bloom and regrow, and tomatoes give several pickings. Crops left standing long after ripening shatter and rot.

The harvest message shows the yield in real units: bushels per acre for grain, hundredweight or tons for vegetables, bales for cotton. Produce is graded **Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent or Premium** by how well the crop was fed, watered and kept healthy.

The grade carries through milling and cooking. Premium food gives 20% more nutrition, Poor food 20% less. Cereals also give **straw**, useful for compost, hay or mulch.
