# Weeds, Glass & Green Manure

**Weeds** come up on every field: fastest on warm, moist, fertile ground, held back by a mulch of residue and shaded out once a crop's canopy closes. They take light, water and nitrogen from the crop. Jade warns when a field is getting weedy.

Right-click a crop with a **hoe** to weed it and its neighbours (a 3x3 patch). Tilling clears weeds too. Rye, oats, clover and alfalfa grown as a **cover crop** smother them.

Sneak-right-click a growing crop with a hoe to cut it down green and work it into the soil as **green manure**: fresh, nitrogen-rich residue, richest from a legume such as clover.

A **glass roof** (glass blocks or panes up to eight blocks above the crop) makes a **greenhouse**: no frost, warmer days and a longer season, but no rain gets in, so water it.
