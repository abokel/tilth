# Tomato

> **Tomato Seeds**  
> **Tomato** (_Solanum lycopersicum_), nightshade family.

- **Warm-season:** sow after the last frost, into warm soil. Sprouts after 70 °C·d above 10 °C.
- Ripens after **1250 °C·d**. Best near 25 °C; stops above 35 °C.
- **Frost** kills it below 0 °C (0.5 °C in flower).
- **Soil pH** 6-6.8 (tolerates 5.5-7.5). Roots 1 m. Dislikes waterlogging.
- **Feeds on** N 150, P 25, K 250 kg/ha per crop.
- **Yield** when well grown: about 27 tons/ac (60 t/ha).
- **Harvest:** Tomato and Tomato Seeds. Regrows after cutting.
