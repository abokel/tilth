# Cabbage

> **Cabbage Seeds**  
> **Cabbage** (_Brassica oleracea var. capitata_), cabbage family.

- **Cool-season:** sow as soon as the ground thaws. Sprouts after 80 °C·d above 4 °C.
- Ripens after **1100 °C·d**. Best near 18 °C; stops above 30 °C.
- **Frost** kills it below -8 °C (-4 °C in flower).
- **Soil pH** 6-7.5 (tolerates 5.5-8). Roots 0.6 m. Some tolerance of wet soil.
- **Feeds on** N 180, P 28, K 200 kg/ha per crop.
- **Yield** when well grown: about 22 tons/ac (50 t/ha).
- **Harvest:** Cabbage and Cabbage Seeds.
