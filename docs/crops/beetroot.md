# Beetroot

> **Beetroot Seeds**  
> **Beetroot** (_Beta vulgaris (table beet)_), beet family.

- **Cool-season:** sow as soon as the ground thaws. Sprouts after 110 °C·d above 4 °C.
- Ripens after **1000 °C·d**. Best near 20 °C; stops above 32 °C.
- **Frost** kills it below -3 °C (-2 °C in flower).
- **Soil pH** 6-7.5 (tolerates 5.5-8). Roots 0.6 m. Some tolerance of wet soil.
- **Feeds on** N 110, P 18, K 160 kg/ha per crop.
- **Yield** when well grown: about 268 cwt/ac (30 t/ha).
- **Harvest:** Beetroot and Beetroot Seeds.
