# Rye

> **Rye Seeds**  
> **Rye** (_Secale cereale_), grass family.

- **Cool-season:** sow as soon as the ground thaws. Sprouts after 100 °C·d above 0 °C.
- Ripens after **1400 °C·d**. Best near 18 °C; stops above 32 °C.
- **Frost** kills it below -20 °C (-2 °C in flower).
- **Soil pH** 5-7 (tolerates 4.5-8). Roots 1.5 m. Some tolerance of wet soil.
- **Feeds on** N 80, P 15, K 75 kg/ha per crop.
- **Yield** when well grown: about 48 bu/ac (3 t/ha).
- **Harvest:** Rye and Rye Seeds.
