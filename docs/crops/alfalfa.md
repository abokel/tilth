# Alfalfa

> **Alfalfa**  
> **Alfalfa** (_Medicago sativa_), legume family.

- **Cool-season:** sow as soon as the ground thaws. Sprouts after 70 °C·d above 5 °C.
- Ripens after **550 °C·d**. Best near 25 °C; stops above 37 °C.
- **Frost** kills it below -20 °C (-3 °C in flower).
- **Soil pH** 6.5-7.5 (tolerates 6-8.5). Roots 1.5 m. Dislikes waterlogging.
- **Feeds on** N 75, P 7, K 55 kg/ha per crop; fixes about 85% of its own nitrogen.
- **Yield** when well grown: about 1 tons/ac (2.5 t/ha).
- **Harvest:** Alfalfa Hay and Alfalfa Seeds. Regrows after cutting.
