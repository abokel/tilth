# Cotton

> **Cotton Seeds**  
> **Cotton** (_Gossypium hirsutum_), mallow family.

- **Warm-season:** sow after the last frost, into warm soil. Sprouts after 40 °C·d above 15.5 °C.
- Ripens after **1150 °C·d**. Best near 30 °C; stops above 40 °C.
- **Frost** kills it below -0.5 °C (0 °C in flower).
- **Soil pH** 5.8-8 (tolerates 5-8.5). Roots 1.3 m. Dislikes waterlogging.
- **Feeds on** N 130, P 22, K 120 kg/ha per crop.
- **Yield** when well grown: about 2 bales/ac (2.5 t/ha).
- **Harvest:** Cotton Boll and Cotton Seeds.
