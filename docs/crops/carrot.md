# Carrot

> **Carrot**  
> **Carrot** (_Daucus carota subsp. sativus_), carrot family.

- **Cool-season:** sow as soon as the ground thaws. Sprouts after 160 °C·d above 4 °C.
- Ripens after **1250 °C·d**. Best near 18 °C; stops above 30 °C.
- **Frost** kills it below -5 °C (-4 °C in flower).
- **Soil pH** 6-6.8 (tolerates 5.5-7.5). Roots 0.6 m. Dislikes waterlogging.
- **Feeds on** N 110, P 20, K 200 kg/ha per crop.
- **Yield** when well grown: about 357 cwt/ac (40 t/ha).
- **Harvest:** Carrot.
