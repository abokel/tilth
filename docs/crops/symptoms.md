# Reading Your Crops

Crops show what they lack:
- **Yellow leaves**, the lower ones first: short of nitrogen (or sulfur or magnesium).
- **Purple leaves**: short of phosphorus, often in cold, wet springs.
- **Dull, drooping leaves**: short of water.

- **Brown, water-soaked edges**: frost damage.

The ground shows its water too: dry farmland cracks, and saturated farmland goes dark with water standing in the furrows. With Jade or WTHIT installed, looking at a crop tells you the same in words.
