# Chickpea

> **Chickpeas**  
> **Chickpea** (_Cicer arietinum_), legume family.

- **Cool-season:** sow as soon as the ground thaws. Sprouts after 80 °C·d above 5 °C.
- Ripens after **1400 °C·d**. Best near 25 °C; stops above 35 °C.
- **Frost** kills it below -4 °C (-1.5 °C in flower).
- **Soil pH** 6-8 (tolerates 5.5-8.8). Roots 0.8 m. Dislikes waterlogging.
- **Feeds on** N 110, P 12, K 60 kg/ha per crop; fixes about 65% of its own nitrogen.
- **Yield** when well grown: about 1606 lb/ac (1.8 t/ha).
- **Harvest:** Chickpeas.
