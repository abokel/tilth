# Durum Wheat

> **Durum Wheat**  
> **Durum Wheat** (_Triticum turgidum subsp. durum_), grass family.

- **Cool-season:** sow as soon as the ground thaws. Sprouts after 110 °C·d above 0 °C.
- Ripens after **1600 °C·d**. Best near 24 °C; stops above 36 °C.
- **Frost** kills it below -10 °C (-2 °C in flower).
- **Soil pH** 6-8 (tolerates 5.5-8.5). Roots 1.5 m. Some tolerance of wet soil.
- **Feeds on** N 110, P 18, K 75 kg/ha per crop.
- **Yield** when well grown: about 45 bu/ac (3 t/ha).
- **Harvest:** Durum Wheat and Durum Wheat Seeds.
