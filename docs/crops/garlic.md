# Garlic

> **Garlic**  
> **Garlic** (_Allium sativum_), onion family.

- **Cool-season:** sow as soon as the ground thaws. Sprouts after 130 °C·d above 0 °C.
- Ripens after **1700 °C·d**. Best near 18 °C; stops above 30 °C.
- **Frost** kills it below -15 °C (-4 °C in flower).
- **Soil pH** 6-7.5 (tolerates 5.5-8). Roots 0.4 m. Dislikes waterlogging.
- **Feeds on** N 120, P 22, K 110 kg/ha per crop.
- **Yield** when well grown: about 89 cwt/ac (10 t/ha).
- **Harvest:** Garlic.
