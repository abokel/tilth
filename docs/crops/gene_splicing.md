# Gene Splicing & the Incubator

> **Gene Splicer**  
> The **Gene Splicer** (iron, redstone and a kitchen knife) makes a controlled cross: craft it with **two seeds of the same crop** into a **Spliced Embryo**. Each parent gives the embryo one copy of each of its genes. The splicer wears a little with each use.

> **Genetic Incubator**  
> Right-click a **Genetic Incubator** with the embryo. Its window glows green while it works; then it holds **two seeds** of the new F1 (right-click to take them). Now and then a gene mutates.

**Breeding a better line** is how real plant breeders work: cross two parents with the traits you want, grow the F1, then save seed and keep the best plants each generation. In a self-pollinating crop the line settles and breeds true in five or six generations.

In cross-pollinating crops, keep two inbred lines apart and cross them for **F1 hybrid** seed each year; the hybrid's own seed is a mixed lot. The travelling merchant sells both: pure lines of wheat and the like, and F1 hybrids of maize and sunflowers.

Very occasionally an embryo grows into something else: a pale, fleshy **homunculus** breaks out of the incubator, slimes about for a few blocks and falls apart. It does no harm, but you lose that embryo.
