# Cultivars & Seed Saving

Every crop is of a **cultivar** with its own traits: how fast it grows, how much it yields, and how well it stands drought and frost. Seed saved from a harvest keeps its cultivar, so you can select the best of your own fields. Now and then a trait mutates.

Crops that need **pollinators** set more seed with flowers nearby and bees working them: keep flower strips and a hive near beans, sunflowers and other insect-pollinated crops.

A **Gene Splicer** combines two seeds into a **Spliced Embryo**, which grows into new seed in a **Genetic Incubator**. Watch it: once in a while something else grows in there, breaks out and slimes about for a few blocks before it falls apart.
