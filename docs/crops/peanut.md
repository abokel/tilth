# Peanut

> **Peanuts**  
> **Peanut** (_Arachis hypogaea_), legume family.

- **Warm-season:** sow after the last frost, into warm soil. Sprouts after 70 °C·d above 10 °C.
- Ripens after **1700 °C·d**. Best near 30 °C; stops above 40 °C.
- **Frost** kills it below -0.5 °C (0 °C in flower).
- **Soil pH** 5.8-6.8 (tolerates 5-7.5). Roots 0.8 m. Dislikes waterlogging.
- **Feeds on** N 160, P 15, K 60 kg/ha per crop; fixes about 65% of its own nitrogen.
- **Yield** when well grown: about 3123 lb/ac (3.5 t/ha).
- **Harvest:** Peanuts.
