# Rice

> **Paddy Rice**  
> **Rice** (_Oryza sativa_), grass family.

- **Warm-season:** sow after the last frost, into warm soil. Sprouts after 60 °C·d above 10 °C.
- Ripens after **1600 °C·d**. Best near 30 °C; stops above 42 °C.
- **Frost** kills it below 2 °C (5 °C in flower).
- **Soil pH** 5.5-7 (tolerates 4.5-8). Roots 0.5 m. Survives flooding.
- **Feeds on** N 100, P 20, K 110 kg/ha per crop.
- **Yield** when well grown: about 40 cwt/ac (4.5 t/ha).
- **Harvest:** Paddy Rice.
