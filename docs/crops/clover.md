# Red Clover

> **Red Clover**  
> **Red Clover** (_Trifolium pratense_), legume family.

- **Cool-season:** sow as soon as the ground thaws. Sprouts after 60 °C·d above 4 °C.
- Ripens after **600 °C·d**. Best near 22 °C; stops above 33 °C.
- **Frost** kills it below -15 °C (-3 °C in flower).
- **Soil pH** 6-7 (tolerates 5.5-7.8). Roots 0.8 m. Some tolerance of wet soil.
- **Feeds on** N 60, P 6, K 50 kg/ha per crop; fixes about 80% of its own nitrogen.
- **Yield** when well grown: about 1 tons/ac (2 t/ha).
- **Harvest:** Red Clover Hay and Clover Seeds. Regrows after cutting.
