# Drying & Storing Grain

Grain comes off the field damp. Above **14%** moisture it goes mouldy in store: dry it first on a **Drying Rack**, which works best in warm, dry weather. The tooltip shows how wet grain is.

Grain kept in chests, barrels or your pockets spoils if it is wet, and rodents and weevils take a little from every chest each day. Losses are worked out when the chest is next opened.

> **Grain Silo Segment**  
> A **Grain Silo** is built from twenty segments as a 2x2 tower five blocks high. Built, it holds over forty thousand grain, dries it by aeration, keeps it four times longer and keeps the rodents out.

Pour grain in with right-click (sneak to pour every sack you carry), take a stack with an empty hand, and sneak with an empty hand to clear spoiled grain. Hoppers fill and empty it, and a comparator reads how full it is. Break the bottom corner and it keeps its grain, like a shulker box.
