# Lentil

> **Lentil**  
> **Lentil** (_Lens culinaris_), legume family.

- **Cool-season:** sow as soon as the ground thaws. Sprouts after 80 °C·d above 5 °C.
- Ripens after **1200 °C·d**. Best near 22 °C; stops above 33 °C.
- **Frost** kills it below -6 °C (-2 °C in flower).
- **Soil pH** 6-8 (tolerates 5.5-8.5). Roots 0.7 m. Dislikes waterlogging.
- **Feeds on** N 100, P 12, K 50 kg/ha per crop; fixes about 70% of its own nitrogen.
- **Yield** when well grown: about 1338 lb/ac (1.5 t/ha).
- **Harvest:** Lentils.
