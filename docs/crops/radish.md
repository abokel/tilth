# Radish

> **Radish Seeds**  
> **Radish** (_Raphanus sativus_), cabbage family.

- **Cool-season:** sow as soon as the ground thaws. Sprouts after 20 °C·d above 2 °C.
- Ripens after **130 °C·d**. Best near 20 °C; stops above 34 °C.
- **Frost** kills it below -6 °C (-5 °C in flower).
- **Soil pH** 5.5-7.5 (tolerates 4.5-8.5). Roots 0.3 m. Some tolerance of wet soil.
- **Feeds on** N 25, P 5, K 35 kg/ha per crop.
- **Yield** when well grown: about 54 cwt/ac (6 t/ha).
- **Harvest:** Radish and Radish Seeds.
