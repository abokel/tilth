# Planting Calendar

**Cool-season crops** grow from 0-5 °C and tolerate light frost. Sow them in early spring, as soon as the ground thaws:

- Alfalfa
- Barley
- Beetroot
- Cabbage
- Carrot
- Chickpea
- Common Wheat
- Durum Wheat
- Field Pea
- Flax
- Garlic
- Lentil
- Lettuce
- Oats
- Radish
- Red Clover
- Rye
- Sugar Beet

**Warm-season crops** need 10 °C or more and die in frost. Sow them after the last frost, in late spring or early summer:

- Common Bean
- Cotton
- Maize
- Onion
- Peanut
- Potato
- Rice
- Sorghum
- Soybean
- Sunflower
- Tomato

**Very hardy crops** survive -10 °C or colder and can be sown in autumn to overwinter:

- Alfalfa
- Barley
- Common Wheat
- Durum Wheat
- Garlic
- Red Clover
- Rye

Check the date with /tilth calendar, and frosts with /tilth climate.
