# Common Wheat

> **Wheat Seeds**  
> **Common Wheat** (_Triticum aestivum_), grass family.

- **Cool-season:** sow as soon as the ground thaws. Sprouts after 100 °C·d above 0 °C.
- Ripens after **1500 °C·d**. Best near 22 °C; stops above 35 °C.
- **Frost** kills it below -12 °C (-2 °C in flower).
- **Soil pH** 6-7.5 (tolerates 5.2-8.5). Roots 1.5 m. Some tolerance of wet soil.
- **Feeds on** N 105, P 18, K 80 kg/ha per crop.
- **Yield** when well grown: about 52 bu/ac (3.5 t/ha).
- **Harvest:** Wheat and Wheat Seeds.
