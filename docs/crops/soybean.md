# Soybean

> **Soybean**  
> **Soybean** (_Glycine max_), legume family.

- **Warm-season:** sow after the last frost, into warm soil. Sprouts after 50 °C·d above 10 °C.
- Ripens after **1250 °C·d**. Best near 28 °C; stops above 40 °C.
- **Frost** kills it below -1.5 °C (-1 °C in flower).
- **Soil pH** 6-7 (tolerates 5.2-8). Roots 1 m. Some tolerance of wet soil.
- **Feeds on** N 230, P 25, K 120 kg/ha per crop; fixes about 60% of its own nitrogen.
- **Yield** when well grown: about 45 bu/ac (3 t/ha).
- **Harvest:** Soybeans.
