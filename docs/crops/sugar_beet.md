# Sugar Beet

> **Sugar Beet**  
> **Sugar Beet** (_Beta vulgaris (sugar beet)_), beet family.

- **Cool-season:** sow as soon as the ground thaws. Sprouts after 120 °C·d above 3 °C.
- Ripens after **2200 °C·d**. Best near 20 °C; stops above 33 °C.
- **Frost** kills it below -4 °C (-3 °C in flower).
- **Soil pH** 6.5-8 (tolerates 6-8.5). Roots 1 m. Some tolerance of wet soil.
- **Feeds on** N 180, P 30, K 250 kg/ha per crop.
- **Yield** when well grown: about 27 tons/ac (60 t/ha).
- **Harvest:** Sugar Beet and Sugar Beet Seeds.
