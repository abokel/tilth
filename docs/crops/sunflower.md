# Sunflower

> **Sunflower**  
> **Sunflower** (_Helianthus annuus_), daisy family.

- **Warm-season:** sow after the last frost, into warm soil. Sprouts after 80 °C·d above 6 °C.
- Ripens after **1500 °C·d**. Best near 25 °C; stops above 38 °C.
- **Frost** kills it below -3 °C (-2 °C in flower).
- **Soil pH** 6-7.5 (tolerates 5.5-8.5). Roots 1.2 m. Dislikes waterlogging.
- **Feeds on** N 110, P 22, K 150 kg/ha per crop.
- **Yield** when well grown: about 2230 lb/ac (2.5 t/ha).
- **Harvest:** Sunflower Seeds. Grows two blocks tall.
