# Sorghum

> **Sorghum Seeds**  
> **Sorghum** (_Sorghum bicolor_), grass family.

- **Warm-season:** sow after the last frost, into warm soil. Sprouts after 60 °C·d above 10 °C.
- Ripens after **1300 °C·d**. Best near 31 °C; stops above 42 °C.
- **Frost** kills it below -1 °C (0 °C in flower).
- **Soil pH** 5.8-7.5 (tolerates 5-8.5). Roots 1.5 m. Some tolerance of wet soil.
- **Feeds on** N 120, P 25, K 120 kg/ha per crop.
- **Yield** when well grown: about 64 bu/ac (4 t/ha).
- **Harvest:** Sorghum Head and Sorghum Seeds. Grows two blocks tall.
