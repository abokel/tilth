# The Soil Lab

> **Soil Lab**  
> The **Soil Lab** is a bench of glassware and reagents. In the field the probe can only tell you how the soil feels, how wet it is and its rough pH.

**Recipe (crafting table, shaped):** 2 × Glass Bottle + Copper Ingot + 3 × any planks + 2 × Stick → Soil Lab

**Sneak and right-click** soil or farmland with the probe to take a **Soil Sample**. Use the sample on the lab; after twenty seconds, right-click the lab to collect a written **Soil Test Report**.

The report has every measurement, the date and place the sample was taken, and advice: how much lime to add, which nutrients are short and what supplies them. Each probe use wears the probe a little; it lasts 63 uses.
