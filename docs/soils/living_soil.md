# Living Soil

Soil you move does not stay the same. Over time it adapts to its new place:
- Subsoil kept wet turns into gley; gley that drains turns back.
- Subsoil exposed to sky and plants slowly becomes topsoil.
- Topsoil buried under rock becomes subsoil.

- Waterlogged topsoil in a cool, wet climate builds up peat.
- Bare topsoil on a slope loses its fine particles in heavy rain, coarsening by one class, and the fines settle in the hollow below.

Grass and crops protect soil from erosion. Plough across slopes, not down them.
