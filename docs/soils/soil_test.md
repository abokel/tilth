# Reading a Soil Test

The lab reports nutrients in **ppm** in the top 20 cm, as a real soil laboratory does. Rough guides:
- **N** (nitrate): under 10 low, 20-40 optimum.
- **P**: under 10 low, 20-40 optimum.
- **K**: under 90 low, 130-200 optimum.

- **pH**: 6.0-7.0 suits most crops.
- **Organic matter**: 3% or more is healthy. It feeds nitrogen and holds water.
- **CEC**: higher means the soil holds nutrients and resists pH change.

**Water** is shown as the volume fraction **θ** next to field capacity (full after drainage) and wilting point (empty to plants). **Compaction** above about 40% restricts roots and drainage; ploughing breaks it up.
