# Horizons & Profiles

Dig down and the soil changes. **Topsoil** (the A horizon) is dark with humus and full of roots. **Subsoil** (the B horizon) is lighter, often redder or yellower, and holds clay washed down from above. Below that lies weathered rock.

> **Gley (Waterlogged Subsoil)**  
> Where the ground stays waterlogged, iron in the subsoil is chemically reduced and turns grey-blue with rusty mottles: **gley**. It marks poorly drained land.

Topsoil is thin on hilltops and steep slopes, where it washes away, and deep on footslopes and valley floors, where it collects. Tilling subsoil gives poor farmland: little humus, little nitrogen.
