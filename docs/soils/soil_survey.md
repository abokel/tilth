# Surveying a Whole Plot

> **Soil Survey**  
> One sample tells you about one spot. A **Soil Survey** collects samples from across a plot and plans for the whole field.

**To file a result:** when the lab has finished, right-click it holding the survey instead of collecting the report. Or hold the survey with a Soil Test Report in your other hand and use it.

A survey belongs to the plot of its first sample; retesting a spot replaces the old result.

**Use the survey** to read it:
- **Plot averages** and the range across samples.
- **Plot plan:** lime and fertiliser per block, and totals for the plot's area in doses and spreader loads.

- **Spots & advice:** samples that differ from the rest and need their own treatment, how many more samples a plot that size needs (about one per 1,000 blocks), and rotation advice from the crop history.
- A page for every sample.
