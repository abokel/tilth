# The Catena

A **catena** is the chain of soils down a hillside. Water, dissolved minerals and fine particles all move downhill, so position on the slope decides the soil.

- **Summit:** stable, moderately deep, well drained.
- **Shoulder:** the most eroded: thin, sandy, low in organic matter.
- **Backslope:** steep and thin.
- **Footslope:** deeper, receives soil from above.
- **Toeslope:** deep, fine-textured, rich in humus.

- **Depression:** a hollow with no outlet. Wet, clayey and gleyed; in cold climates it fills with peat.
- **Floodplain:** silty river sediment over a shallow water table.

The parent material (wind-blown loess, glacial till, river alluvium, lake clay, dune sand) sets the starting texture.

Use **/tilth catena** anywhere to see the slope, curvature, relief, hillslope position and the soil profile the land implies. Worldgen builds every new chunk this way.
