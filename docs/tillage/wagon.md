# Farm Wagon

> **Farm Wagon**  
> The **Farm Wagon** carries as much as a chest. Hitch it like the plough, from the saddle of a horse, donkey or mule, and it follows your team.

From the ground, right-click it to load or unload it, and sneak-right-click to unhitch. From the saddle, right-click sets or releases the brake. A full wagon is heavier to pull than an empty one.
