# Compaction & Wet Clay

Feet, hooves and landing on farmland compact it, and wet soil compacts far more. Compacted soil drains poorly, runs off and stunts roots. Freeze-thaw cycles slowly loosen it, and tillage breaks it at once.

Never work **wet clay**: the plough smears it into hard clods and makes compaction worse. Wait until the soil has dried below field capacity. The Soil Probe shows the moisture.
