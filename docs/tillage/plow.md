# The Moldboard Plough

A three-bottom moldboard plough. It turns a three-block swath, buries residue and breaks compaction.

**Recipe (crafting table, shaped):** Stick + 3 × any logs + 2 × Iron Ingot → Moldboard Plough

**To plough:** place it, mount a horse, donkey or mule, and right-click the plough to hitch it. You can also hitch it to yourself on foot, but it is slow work.

**Sneak and right-click** to lower or raise the shares. Lowered, it tills everything it is dragged over.

Draft resistance slows whatever is pulling. Raise the shares to travel, and right-click again to unhitch.

Ploughing destroys crops in its path and turns them into residue. Hit the plough to pick it up.
