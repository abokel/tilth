# Fertilizer Spreader

A horse-drawn broadcast spreader with a spinning disc.

**Recipe (crafting table, shaped):** 4 × any planks + Hopper + 2 × Stick + Iron Ingot → Fertilizer Spreader

**Load it** by right-clicking with manure, compost, lime, wood ash, sulfur, bone meal or straw. The hopper holds 128 of one kind.

Hitch it like the plough, then **right-click it again from the saddle** to open or close the hopper.

Pulled across a field, it spreads one dose on every block of a three-block swath, under growing crops too. Opening or closing it tells you what is left. Break it to get the load back.
