# Hoe & Ard

A **hoe** tills one block. Tilling vanilla dirt or grass gives farmland of whatever soil the terrain implies there.

> **Ard (Scratch Plough)**  
> The **Ard** is the oldest plough: a pointed beam dragged by hand. It stirs a three-block strip ahead of you without turning the soil over. That is quick, but it buries little residue and loosens compaction only partly.

**Recipe (crafting table, shaped):** 2 × Stick + any logs + Flint → Ard (Scratch Plough)
