# Irrigation & Water Table

Fields draw water three ways:
- **Rain:** what doesn't run off a slope soaks in.
- **Seepage** from nearby water: two blocks through sand, four through loam, five or more through silt, clay and peat.
- **Capillary rise** from a shallow water table.

The water table is shallow in valleys and near rivers and deep on hilltops, so lower slopes stay moist through dry spells.

Crops use water fastest when the weather is hot, dry and windy, and when they are fully grown.

**Rice** grows best flooded: plant it with water directly beside the block. Other crops drown if the soil stays saturated for too long.
