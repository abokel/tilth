# White Mold

**White Mold** (_Sclerotinia sclerotiorum_), fungus.

- **Hosts:** soybean, common bean, pea, chickpea, lentil, peanut, sunflower, lettuce, cabbage, carrot, potato, tomato.
- **Spreads:** soilborne, airborne spores.
- **Favoured by** 15-25 °C (best 20 °C), humidity above 90%, 16 h of wet leaves.

- **Survives** 5 years (sclerotia in soil).
- **Damage:** up to 50% yield, can kill the plant.
- **Control:** Rotate with cereals; Wider rows for airflow; Biological control (Coniothyrium minitans).

Hundreds of host species: rotate with grasses, not with other broadleaf crops.
