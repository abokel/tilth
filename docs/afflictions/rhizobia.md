# Rhizobia (Nodule Bacteria)

**Rhizobia (Nodule Bacteria)** (_Rhizobium, Bradyrhizobium and Sinorhizobium spp._), symbiont.

- **Hosts:** fabaceae.
- **Spreads:** soilborne, seedborne.
- **Favoured by** 10-35 °C (best 25 °C), pH below 8.5, pH above 5.5.

- **Survives** 5 years (soil).
- **Benefit:** +80% to its host.
- **Control:** Inoculate seed when a legume is new to the field; Lime acid soils above pH 6.

Fix atmospheric nitrogen for the legume; the residue then feeds the next crop.
