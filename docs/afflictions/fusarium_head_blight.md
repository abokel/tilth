# Fusarium Head Blight (Scab)

**Fusarium Head Blight (Scab)** (_Fusarium graminearum_), fungus.

- **Hosts:** common wheat, durum wheat, barley, oats, rye, maize.
- **Spreads:** crop residue, airborne spores, rain splash.
- **Favoured by** 15-32 °C (best 27 °C), humidity above 90%, 24 h of wet leaves.

- **Survives** 2 years (residue).
- **Damage:** up to 50% yield.
- **Control:** Do not sow wheat into maize residue; Plough under infected residue; Fungicide at anthesis; Moderately resistant cultivars.

Contaminates grain with the mycotoxin deoxynivalenol (DON, 'vomitoxin').
