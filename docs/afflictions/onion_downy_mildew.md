# Onion Downy Mildew

**Onion Downy Mildew** (_Peronospora destructor_), oomycete.

- **Hosts:** onion, garlic.
- **Spreads:** airborne spores, seedborne.
- **Favoured by** 4-22 °C (best 13 °C), humidity above 95%, 7 h of wet leaves.

- **Survives** 1 years (infected bulbs).
- **Damage:** up to 50% yield.
- **Control:** Healthy sets; Wide spacing; Rotation.
