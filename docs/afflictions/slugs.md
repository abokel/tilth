# Slugs

**Slugs** (_Deroceras reticulatum_), mollusc.

- **Hosts:** all crops.
- **Spreads:** migration.
- **Favoured by** 5-22 °C (best 15 °C), humidity above 90%.

- **Survives** 1 years (eggs in residue).
- **Damage:** up to 25% yield, can kill the plant.
- **Control:** Firm, fine seedbed; Tillage; Beetles and birds; Ferric phosphate bait.
