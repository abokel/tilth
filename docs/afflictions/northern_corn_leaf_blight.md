# Northern Corn Leaf Blight

**Northern Corn Leaf Blight** (_Exserohilum turcicum_), fungus.

- **Hosts:** maize, sorghum.
- **Spreads:** crop residue, airborne spores.
- **Favoured by** 18-27 °C (best 24 °C), humidity above 85%, 6 h of wet leaves.

- **Survives** 1 years (residue).
- **Damage:** up to 40% yield.
- **Control:** Rotation; Plough down residue; Resistant hybrids (Ht genes).
