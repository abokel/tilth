# Trichoderma

**Trichoderma** (_Trichoderma harzianum_), symbiont.

- **Hosts:** all crops.
- **Spreads:** soilborne.
- **Favoured by** 10-35 °C (best 25 °C).

- **Survives** 3 years (soil).
- **Benefit:** +15% to its host.
- **Control:** Organic matter and compost encourage it.

A soil fungus that parasitises other fungi; used as a biological control agent.
