# Western Corn Rootworm

**Western Corn Rootworm** (_Diabrotica virgifera virgifera_), insect.

- **Hosts:** maize.
- **Spreads:** soilborne.
- **Favoured by** 12-32 °C (best 24 °C).

- **Survives** 1 years (eggs in soil).
- **Damage:** up to 40% yield.
- **Control:** Rotate maize with soybean; Bt rootworm hybrids.

A classic case for crop rotation; a rotation-resistant strain now lays eggs in soybean fields.
