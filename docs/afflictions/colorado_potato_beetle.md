# Colorado Potato Beetle

**Colorado Potato Beetle** (_Leptinotarsa decemlineata_), insect.

- **Hosts:** potato, tomato.
- **Spreads:** migration, soilborne.
- **Favoured by** 15-32 °C (best 26 °C).

- **Survives** 1 years (adults in soil).
- **Damage:** up to 60% yield, can kill the plant.
- **Control:** Rotate fields far apart; Hand-pick adults and egg masses; Straw mulch; Bt tenebrionis.

Famous for evolving resistance to almost every insecticide used against it.
