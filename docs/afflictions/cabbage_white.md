# Cabbage White Butterfly

**Cabbage White Butterfly** (_Pieris rapae_), insect.

- **Hosts:** brassicaceae.
- **Spreads:** migration.
- **Favoured by** 15-30 °C (best 24 °C).

- **Survives** 0.5 years (pupae).
- **Damage:** up to 30% yield.
- **Control:** Insect netting; Hand-pick caterpillars; Bt kurstaki.
