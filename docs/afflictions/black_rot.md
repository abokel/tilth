# Black Rot of Crucifers

**Black Rot of Crucifers** (_Xanthomonas campestris pv. campestris_), bacterium.

- **Hosts:** brassicaceae.
- **Spreads:** seedborne, rain splash, crop residue.
- **Favoured by** 15-35 °C (best 27 °C), humidity above 85%, 4 h of wet leaves.

- **Survives** 1 years (residue).
- **Damage:** up to 50% yield.
- **Control:** Hot-water treated seed; Rotation; Avoid overhead irrigation.
