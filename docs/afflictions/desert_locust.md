# Desert Locust

**Desert Locust** (_Schistocerca gregaria_), insect.

- **Hosts:** all crops.
- **Spreads:** migration.
- **Favoured by** 25-38 °C (best 32 °C).

- **Survives** 0 years (migration).
- **Damage:** up to 100% yield, can kill the plant.
- **Control:** Regional early warning; Targeted biopesticide (Metarhizium).

Solitary grasshoppers turn gregarious after rains; a single swarm can eat as much as millions of people in a day.
