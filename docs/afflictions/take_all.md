# Take-all

**Take-all** (_Gaeumannomyces tritici_), fungus.

- **Hosts:** common wheat, durum wheat, barley.
- **Spreads:** soilborne, crop residue.
- **Favoured by** 10-25 °C (best 18 °C), pH below 8.5, pH above 6.

- **Survives** 1 years (residue).
- **Damage:** up to 50% yield, can kill the plant.
- **Control:** One-year break with a non-grass crop; Avoid over-liming; Ammonium rather than nitrate nitrogen; Take-all decline after continuous wheat.

Worse in alkaline, freshly limed, loose or waterlogged soils.
