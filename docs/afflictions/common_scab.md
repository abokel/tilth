# Common Scab

**Common Scab** (_Streptomyces scabiei_), bacterium.

- **Hosts:** potato, carrot, beetroot, sugar beet.
- **Spreads:** soilborne, seedborne.
- **Favoured by** 10-30 °C (best 22 °C), pH above 5.3.

- **Survives** 10 years (soil).
- **Damage:** up to 5% yield.
- **Control:** Keep soil pH below 5.3; Irrigate during tuber initiation; Avoid fresh manure and liming before potatoes.

A cosmetic disease that ruins market quality. Strongly suppressed by acidic soil.
