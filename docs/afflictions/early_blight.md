# Early Blight

**Early Blight** (_Alternaria solani_), fungus.

- **Hosts:** potato, tomato.
- **Spreads:** crop residue, airborne spores, rain splash.
- **Favoured by** 15-32 °C (best 26 °C), humidity above 80%, 6 h of wet leaves.

- **Survives** 1 years (residue).
- **Damage:** up to 30% yield.
- **Control:** Two-year rotation away from nightshades; Plough under residue; Avoid plant stress (water, nitrogen); Fungicide.
