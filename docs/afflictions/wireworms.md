# Wireworms

**Wireworms** (_Elateridae (click beetle larvae)_), insect.

- **Hosts:** all crops.
- **Spreads:** soilborne.
- **Favoured by** 10-25 °C (best 18 °C).

- **Survives** 4 years (larvae in soil).
- **Damage:** up to 30% yield.
- **Control:** Avoid planting into freshly broken grass sod; Cultivate in late summer; Bait stations.

Worst in the first years after ploughing up old pasture.
