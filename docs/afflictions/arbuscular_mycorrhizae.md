# Arbuscular Mycorrhizal Fungi

**Arbuscular Mycorrhizal Fungi** (_Glomeromycota_), symbiont.

- **Hosts:** poaceae, fabaceae, solanaceae, amaryllidaceae, apiaceae, asteraceae, linaceae, malvaceae.
- **Spreads:** soilborne, root contact.
- **Favoured by** 10-35 °C (best 25 °C).

- **Survives** 2 years (spores and hyphae in soil).
- **Benefit:** +30% to its host.
- **Control:** Reduced tillage; Avoid long bare fallows; Avoid very high phosphorus.

Brassicas and beets are non-hosts, so a cabbage or sugar beet crop lets the network decline.
