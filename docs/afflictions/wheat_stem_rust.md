# Wheat Stem Rust

**Wheat Stem Rust** (_Puccinia graminis f. sp. tritici_), fungus.

- **Hosts:** common wheat, durum wheat, barley, rye.
- **Spreads:** airborne spores.
- **Favoured by** 15-35 °C (best 25 °C), humidity above 85%, 6 h of wet leaves.

- **Survives** 0.5 years (alternate host).
- **Damage:** up to 70% yield.
- **Control:** Resistant cultivars (Sr genes); Eradicate barberry, the alternate host; Early sowing; Fungicide at flag leaf.

The Ug99 lineage, found in Uganda in 1999, defeated the widely used Sr31 resistance gene.
