# The Affliction Model

Thirty-one organisms are modelled from real plant pathology: rusts, blights, scab, clubroot, viruses, aphids, beetles, rootworm, nematodes, slugs, and helpful mycorrhizae, rhizobia and Trichoderma.

Each one has its hosts, the weather it likes (temperature, humidity, hours of wet leaves), soil conditions, how it spreads, how long it survives without a host, the damage it does and how farmers control it.

They are **off by default** while their visuals are made.

Turn them on with afflictions.enabled in the config. **/tilth affliction list** and **info** describe each organism, **risk** shows today's infection risk for the crop you are looking at, and **infect** / **clear** let you test them.

To see what is attacking a crop, cut a **Plant Sample** from it with the **Kitchen Knife** (right-click the crop), hold the sample in your off hand and use a **Microscope**. It shows the organism magnified, with its symptoms and how to control it.

**Recipe (crafting table, shaped):** Glass Pane + 2 × Iron Ingot + 2 × Copper Ingot → Microscope

Rotation is the main defence. Soil- and residue-borne organisms build up when the same host is grown again before they die out. Ploughing buries residue-borne ones.
