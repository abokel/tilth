# Powdery Mildew of Cereals

**Powdery Mildew of Cereals** (_Blumeria graminis_), fungus.

- **Hosts:** common wheat, durum wheat, barley, oats, rye.
- **Spreads:** airborne spores.
- **Favoured by** 5-25 °C (best 18 °C), humidity above 70%.

- **Survives** 0.5 years (residue).
- **Damage:** up to 25% yield.
- **Control:** Resistant cultivars; Avoid excessive nitrogen and dense stands; Fungicide.

Unlike most leaf diseases it does not need free water; dry, mild, humid weather favours it.
