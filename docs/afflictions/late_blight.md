# Late Blight

**Late Blight** (_Phytophthora infestans_), oomycete.

- **Hosts:** potato, tomato.
- **Spreads:** airborne spores, rain splash, seedborne.
- **Favoured by** 10-25 °C (best 18 °C), humidity above 90%, 10 h of wet leaves.

- **Survives** 0.2 years (infected tubers).
- **Damage:** up to 100% yield, can kill the plant.
- **Control:** Certified disease-free seed potatoes; Destroy cull piles and volunteers; Protectant fungicide programme; Resistant varieties.

Caused the Irish Potato Famine of 1845-1849.
