# Gray Mold

**Gray Mold** (_Botrytis cinerea_), fungus.

- **Hosts:** tomato, lettuce, pea, common bean, sunflower, onion, cabbage.
- **Spreads:** airborne spores.
- **Favoured by** 15-25 °C (best 20 °C), humidity above 90%, 8 h of wet leaves.

- **Survives** 1 years (residue).
- **Damage:** up to 30% yield.
- **Control:** Ventilation; Remove infected tissue; Avoid late overhead watering.
