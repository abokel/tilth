# Soybean Cyst Nematode

**Soybean Cyst Nematode** (_Heterodera glycines_), nematode.

- **Hosts:** soybean, common bean.
- **Spreads:** soilborne, mechanical contact.
- **Favoured by** 15-30 °C (best 25 °C), pH above 6.

- **Survives** 10 years (cysts in soil).
- **Damage:** up to 30% yield.
- **Control:** Rotate with non-hosts (maize, wheat); Resistant varieties (PI 88788, Peking).

Often causes yield loss with no visible symptoms.
