# Tomato Mosaic

**Tomato Mosaic** (_Tomato mosaic virus (Tobamovirus)_), virus.

- **Hosts:** tomato.
- **Spreads:** mechanical contact, seedborne.
- **Favoured by** 15-32 °C (best 25 °C).

- **Survives** 2 years (seed and residue).
- **Damage:** up to 25% yield.
- **Control:** Resistant varieties (Tm-2 genes); Wash hands and tools; Clean seed.

One of the most stable viruses known; survives for years in dried plant debris.
