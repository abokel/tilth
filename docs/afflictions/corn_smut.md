# Corn Smut

**Corn Smut** (_Ustilago maydis_), fungus.

- **Hosts:** maize.
- **Spreads:** airborne spores, soilborne.
- **Favoured by** 26-34 °C (best 30 °C).

- **Survives** 3 years (soil).
- **Damage:** up to 10% yield.
- **Control:** Avoid wounding plants; Balanced fertility.

The galls are eaten as huitlacoche, a delicacy in Mexican cuisine.
