# Barley Yellow Dwarf

**Barley Yellow Dwarf** (_Barley yellow dwarf virus (Luteoviridae)_), virus.

- **Hosts:** common wheat, durum wheat, barley, oats, rye, maize, rice, sorghum.
- **Spreads:** insect vector.
- **Favoured by** 10-28 °C (best 20 °C).

- **Survives** 0.5 years (aphids on grasses).
- **Damage:** up to 40% yield.
- **Control:** Delay autumn sowing until aphid flights end; Control aphids; Tolerant varieties.

Spread only by aphids.
