# Damping-off

**Damping-off** (_Pythium spp._), oomycete.

- **Hosts:** all crops.
- **Spreads:** soilborne.
- **Favoured by** 10-25 °C (best 15 °C).

- **Survives** 5 years (soil).
- **Damage:** up to 100% yield, can kill the plant.
- **Control:** Sow into warm, well-drained soil; Avoid overwatering seedbeds; Treated seed.
