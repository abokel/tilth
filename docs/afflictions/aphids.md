# Aphids

**Aphids** (_Aphidoidea_), insect.

- **Hosts:** all crops.
- **Spreads:** migration.
- **Favoured by** 10-30 °C (best 22 °C).

- **Survives** 0.5 years (eggs on woody hosts).
- **Damage:** up to 20% yield.
- **Control:** Conserve lady beetles, lacewings and hoverflies; Insecticidal soap; Avoid excess nitrogen.

Vectors of many plant viruses, including barley yellow dwarf.
