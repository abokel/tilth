# Clubroot

**Clubroot** (_Plasmodiophora brassicae_), protist.

- **Hosts:** brassicaceae.
- **Spreads:** soilborne, mechanical contact.
- **Favoured by** 13-30 °C (best 23 °C), pH below 7.2.

- **Survives** 15 years (resting spores in soil).
- **Damage:** up to 80% yield, can kill the plant.
- **Control:** Lime to pH 7.2 or higher; Long rotation away from brassicas; Clean soil off boots and tools; Resistant varieties.

Resting spores survive in soil for 15 years or more; liming is the classic control.
