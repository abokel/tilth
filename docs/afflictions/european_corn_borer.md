# European Corn Borer

**European Corn Borer** (_Ostrinia nubilalis_), insect.

- **Hosts:** maize, sorghum.
- **Spreads:** migration, crop residue.
- **Favoured by** 15-32 °C (best 25 °C).

- **Survives** 1 years (larvae in stalks).
- **Damage:** up to 20% yield.
- **Control:** Plough down maize stalks; Bt hybrids; Trichogramma wasps.
