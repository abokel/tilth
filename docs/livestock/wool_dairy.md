# Wool & Dairy

Shearing gives graded **fleece** in the sheep's colour: a Merino's is the finest and most, a thin or sick sheep's the poorest. Spin it into **yarn** on a **Spinning Wheel** (sneak to felt it into wool instead), and weave four yarn into **Woollen Cloth**.

Milk is graded too, richest from a Jersey in good condition. A **Butter Churn** turns a bucket of milk into butter at once; a **Cheese Press** takes up to four buckets and ages them into cheese over three days. Graded produce fetches more at market.
