# Animal Condition

Cows, sheep, goats, pigs, chickens and horses each have a **condition**, from starving to top condition. It falls steadily, from top condition to starving in about two days without food.

Animals put on condition by **grazing** grass (heavy grazing wears pasture down to bare soil), by eating from a **hay bale** within two blocks (a bale feeds about ten meals), or when you feed them by hand: wheat, hay, oats, barley, maize, alfalfa or clover.

> **Alfalfa Hay Bale**  
> An **Alfalfa Hay Bale** (nine alfalfa) is far richer than grass hay: each meal does half again as much good, and a bale lasts about fourteen meals.

Grass barely grows in cold weather, so in winter grazing gives little and animals need hay. Use **/tilth animal @n** or Jade to see an animal's condition.
