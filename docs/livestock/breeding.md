# Sexes & Breeding

Every animal is **male or female**. Males show it: bulls have a nose ring and heavy horns, rams curled horns, boars tusks, roosters a tall comb and a tail plume. Jade and /tilth animal tell you too.

Only a **male and a female** will breed, and both must be in good condition. **Sheep and goats** only come into season in autumn, from September to December.

Mammals carry their young before giving birth: about **four days** for mares, **three** for cows and **two** for ewes, nannies and sows. Sows have litters of two to four; ewes and nannies often twins. Giving birth costs the mother condition.
