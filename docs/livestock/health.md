# Pasture & Animal Health

Grazing wears a pasture down. Move animals between paddocks so the grass can recover: a rested pasture feeds them far better than a bare one. Jade shows how good the grazing is underfoot.

Animals can fall sick, more easily when thin, crowded or on worn pasture: **bovine respiratory disease** in cattle, **swine influenza** in pigs and **coccidiosis** in poultry. Sick animals lose condition, give less and breed less.

Look at a sick animal through the **microscope** (right-click it) to diagnose it. A **Veterinary Draught** then treats it, far better soon after a diagnosis. Sneak-right-click with a **lead** to quarantine it: a quarantined animal spreads disease far less, but neither breeds nor grazes, so feed it by hand.
