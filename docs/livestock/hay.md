# Making Hay

Cut grass with a **hoe** for **Cut Grass**, and lay it on the ground in a **windrow**. Over about a game day of fair weather it wilts, cures and dries; turn it with a hoe to hurry it along.

Rain on hay that has started to cure can spoil it into **mouldy hay**, only fit for compost. Gather dry windrows for **Dried Hay**: nine make a hay bale, and animals eat it too.
