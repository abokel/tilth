# Milk, Eggs & Meat

- **Breeding:** a thin animal puts feed into condition instead of breeding.
- **Milk:** cows and nannies give milk once a day, and only when not thin.
- **Eggs:** thin hens stop laying; roosters never lay.

- **Wool:** sheep regrow wool by grazing.
- **Manure:** well-fed animals leave dung on the ground to shovel up.
- **Meat:** yield and quality follow condition, from a scrap of Poor meat to Premium cuts.
