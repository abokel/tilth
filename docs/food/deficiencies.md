# Deficiencies

- **Scurvy** (vitamin C): wounds heal at half speed and you mine slower.
- **Anemia** (iron): you move slower.
- **Night Blindness** (vitamin A): darkness closes in.
- **Brittle Bones** (calcium): falls hurt 50% more.

- **Protein Deficiency:** weaker attacks.
- **Low Energy** (carbohydrate or fat): slower, and you tire faster.
- **Digestive Distress** (fibre): you get hungry faster.
- **Malnourished:** an overall poor diet costs two hearts.

Milk does not cure them: only eating the missing nutrient does.
