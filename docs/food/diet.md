# Your Diet

Your body keeps stores of eight nutrients: carbohydrate, protein, fat, fibre, vitamin A, vitamin C, iron and calcium. Energy runs out within a day or two, while vitamins A, iron and calcium last many days. Check yours with **/tilth diet**.

Every food tooltip shows what it provides (hold Shift for the full list). Eat a variety: grains for energy, legumes and meat for protein and iron, vegetables for vitamins, milk and tofu for calcium.

A balanced diet makes you **Well Nourished**: two extra hearts.
