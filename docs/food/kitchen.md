# The Kitchen

Grind grain into flour with the quern, mix doughs with a water bucket, cut pasta and fries with the knife, then cook them in a furnace, smoker or campfire. Better ingredients make better food: the grade carries through.

**Recipe (crafting table):** Durum Wheat + Quern → Durum Semolina

Durum wheat is the pasta wheat.

**Recipe (crafting table):** 2 × Durum Semolina + Water Bucket → 2 × Pasta Dough

**Recipe (crafting table):** Durum Semolina + Chickpea Flour + Egg → 2 × Protein Pasta Dough

Chickpea or lentil flour turns pasta into a protein meal.

**Recipe (furnace, smoker or campfire):** Raw Protein Pasta → Protein Pasta

**Recipe (crafting table):** 2 × Cornmeal + Water Bucket + Wood Ash → 2 × Masa Dough

Cornmeal cooked with wood ash (nixtamalisation) becomes masa for tortillas.

**Recipe (crafting table):** 2 × Tortilla + Bowl of Rice + Bowl of Beans + Tomato → 2 × Burrito

**Recipe (crafting table):** 2 × Chickpeas + Garlic + Bottle of Vegetable Oil + Bowl → Hummus

**Recipe (crafting table):** 2 × Lentils + Onion + Carrot + Bowl → Lentil Soup
