# Composting

> **Compost Bin**  
> A **Compost Bin** turns farm waste into compost. Put in **greens** (manure, cut grass, spoiled grain, mouldy hay, scraps) and **browns** (straw, dried hay, leaves, sticks).

About **two browns to one green** rots fastest, in roughly three days. An all-green heap goes slimy and an all-brown one barely rots. **Turn** it with a shovel, up to three times, to speed it up. Right-click with an empty hand to dig out the compost: half the volume that went in.
