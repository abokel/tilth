# Nutrients

Plants take six nutrients from the soil:
- **Nitrogen (N):** leaves and protein. Mobile: washes out with drainage, worst in sand.
- **Phosphorus (P):** roots and seed. Locked up in very acid or very alkaline soil.
- **Potassium (K):** water use and quality. Held by clay.

- **Sulfur, Calcium, Magnesium:** needed in smaller amounts; calcium matters for peanuts and tomatoes.

Growth is limited by whichever nutrient is shortest (Liebig's law of the minimum). Adding more of the others does nothing.

Each crop needs a different amount: maize and cabbage are heavy feeders, while legumes make most of their own nitrogen. Micronutrients (iron, zinc, manganese) become scarce above pH 7.5, and sensitive crops then yellow.
