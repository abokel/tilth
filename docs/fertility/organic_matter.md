# Organic Matter & Residue

Humus (soil organic matter) slowly breaks down and releases nitrogen. This happens fastest in warm, moist soil and right after tillage, which brings a short flush of fertility.

Crop residue left on the field becomes new humus over time.

Residue type matters. **Straw** is low in nitrogen, so soil microbes borrow nitrogen from the soil to digest it, and the next crop may go short. **Legume residue** (clover, alfalfa, beans) is rich in nitrogen and gives it back.

Mulch left on the surface protects soil from drying out. Ploughed in, residue decomposes much faster.
