# Soil pH

pH controls how much of each nutrient plants can actually reach. Rain slowly acidifies soil, especially in humid climates, and every soil drifts back toward its climate's natural pH over the years, so liming must be repeated.

> **Agricultural Lime**  
> **Agricultural Lime** raises pH. Grind calcite or dripstone in a quern. Clay and humus-rich soils need more lime per step.

**Recipe (crafting table):** Calcite + Quern → 4 × Agricultural Lime

> **Elemental Sulfur**  
> **Elemental Sulfur** lowers pH, for potatoes (it reduces scab) and acid-loving crops.

Crop preferences:
- **Alfalfa, sugar beet:** 6.5-8.
- **Most crops:** 6-7.
- **Potato, oats, rye:** 5-6.5.

Liming to 7.2 also controls clubroot in cabbage-family crops.
