# Crop Rotation

Farmland remembers its last six crops. Rotating crop families breaks the cycles of soil-borne diseases and pests (take-all in wheat, rootworm in maize, clubroot in cabbage).

A classic rotation is **grain → legume → root crop → grain**. Legumes leave nitrogen behind. Root crops loosen the soil. Grasses after grasses invite disease.

Cover crops of clover over winter protect and feed the soil.
