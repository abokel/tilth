# Amendments

> **Bucket of Slurry**  
> **Slurry** is manure mixed with water. Animals leave dung on the ground in layers: **shovel** it up, then craft four manure with a water bucket into a **Bucket of Slurry**.

Pour slurry on farmland for one dose on every block of a 3x3 patch, or load buckets into the fertiliser spreader (nine doses each). It supplies nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium and organic matter.

Dung left lying slowly rots into the ground beneath it.

> **Compost**  
> **Compost** is slower and gentler. It builds organic matter and supplies balanced nutrients.

**Recipe (crafting table):** Manure + 2 × Straw + any dirt → 3 × Compost

> **Bone Meal**  
> **Bone meal** is a phosphorus and calcium fertiliser here. It no longer makes crops grow instantly (configurable).

> **Wood Ash**  
> **Wood Ash**, from burning sticks, adds potassium and calcium and raises pH.

**Recipe (furnace, smoker or campfire):** Stick → Wood Ash

Right-click farmland (or a crop on it) with an amendment to spread one dose per block. Alfalfa and clover hay work as **green manure**: nitrogen-rich residue for the next crop.
