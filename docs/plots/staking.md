# Staking a Field

Hold the **Surveyor's Chain** and right-click the corner blocks of your field in order, walking around it. Any shape works: rectangles, L-shapes, a field following a river. Sneak and right-click a block to remove the last stake.

**Sneak and use the chain in the air** to record the plot. Rename the chain in an anvil first to name the plot.

While you hold the chain, stakes and plot boundaries are shown in particles. Use it in the air without sneaking to see which plot you are standing in.

**Recipe (crafting table):** 2 × Chain + 2 × Stick + Paper → Surveyor's Chain
