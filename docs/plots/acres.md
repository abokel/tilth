# Acres & Areas

One block is one square metre, so an acre is about 4,047 blocks. A 64 by 64 field (four by four chunks) is almost exactly one acre.

The area counts every block inside or on the boundary, so staking the corners of a 10 by 10 field gives exactly 100 m².

Gunter's chain, 66 feet long, was the surveyor's tool that defined the acre: ten square chains.

**/tilth plot info** reports a plot's area, perimeter, average soil test and the crops growing in it.
